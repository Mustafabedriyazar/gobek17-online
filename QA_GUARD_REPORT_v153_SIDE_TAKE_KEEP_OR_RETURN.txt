USER-LOCKED RULE
- Side-taken tile penalty is always represented tile value x10 to the taker.
- The exact side-taken tile may NOT be discarded to the right.
- Legal resolutions:
  A) use it in legal OPEN/PER/PROCESS => x10,
  B) return exact tile to source/left discard => x10, turn ends,
  C) keep it in rack and discard ANOTHER rack tile => x10, normal turn end.
- TAKE_CANCEL = return + x10; no free escape.

ROOT CAUSE
v152 a_discard() treated any discard attempt while pending as an implicit TAKE_RETURN_PENALTY transaction. That forced the side tile back to source and prevented the new keep-in-rack + discard-other option. v153 splits pending resolution into explicit return and keep paths, and rejects pending.uid as a right-side discard.
